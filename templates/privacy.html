Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you create an account, we collect your name and email address. As you use Spendly, we store the expense information you enter, such as amounts, categories, dates and descriptions. We do not collect payment card details or connect to your bank accounts.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data only to operate Spendly: to sign you in, to save and display your expenses, and to generate the summaries and breakdowns you see in the app. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely and is accessible only through your account. Passwords are stored in hashed form, never as plain text. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time, and you may ask us to delete your account and the data associated with it.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when a page loads. We do not share your expense data with any third party, except where required by law.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact the Spendly team through the contact details provided on the website.
